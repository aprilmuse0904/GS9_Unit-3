PART C · GRAMMAR

First Conditional + Modal Verbs & Suggestions

First Conditional

If + present simple, S + will / can / may / might / should + V₀.

If you get enough sleep, you may feel better.

Unless = if ... not: Unless you study, you may fail.

Suggest

suggest + V-ing (including not + V-ing)

I suggest taking a break.

suggest (that) + S + (should) + V₀

She suggested that I should rest.

Note: A suggestion is not always exactly the same as an instruction or strong warning. The rewrites below practise natural suggestions in context. Alternative correct answers may be accepted by your teacher.

PART D · FINAL PRODUCTION

My healthy living presentation

Open with a question: “Do you ever feel tired or stressed because of schoolwork? Today, I'd like to share three ways to have a healthier life.”

Set a scene: “Imagine you have three tests tomorrow and only a few hours to study. What should you do?”

Speaking checklist: clear opening · three connected ideas · five target expressions · one first conditional · clear closing.
